# Brag Plan: Yummy Menu — One Complete Restaurant Visit

## What is this app?
Yummy Menu is the customer-facing restaurant experience that connects table QR verification, menu browsing, table ordering, service requests, and a returning customer profile.

## The angle
One continuous visit, not a feature reel. A single orange journey line visibly carries the customer from the table QR through every product action and closes around Yummy points. Each screen is caused by the action before it.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
A warm, tactile table scene: the phone lifts toward a privacy-safe Yummy table QR while “Your table is ready.” settles in the upper-left. The four orange scanner corners introduce the journey line.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The verified table resolves into the real dark Yummy restaurant header and “Everything for your visit, in one place.”
- The customer moves from Momo to Chicken momo to Extra spicy, then places the active-table order.
- The same visit continues through Request Bill and settles into Orders, Yummy points, and Rewards.

## Outro / punchline
The journey line closes into an orange dot beside the real Yummy logo: “Dine, earn, return.”

## User flow worth showing
Scan table QR → browse and customize Chicken momo → place the table order → request the bill → see the visit saved with profile, points, and rewards context.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm premium hospitality with operational clarity
- Interpretation: Calm camera movement, fast but restrained interactions, readable holds, no decorative spectacle, and no disconnected feature montage.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080 at 30 fps
## Duration: exactly 25.00 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project and supplied brief)
- Background: `#f6f6f4`
- Accent: `#ff6929`
- Text: `#10131a`
- Surface: `#fffdf9`
- Display font: Outfit
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the real dark restaurant shell, compact menu rows, orange interaction states, and the continuous orange journey line

## Share copy (draft)
One table. One connected visit. Yummy Menu takes the guest from QR to order, bill, points and rewards — dine, earn, return.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional interaction accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: low-volume fade-in under the restaurant ambience; purposeful middle; 0.8-second fade into the final lockup
- Music cue guidance: bundled 109.96 BPM preset; use 8.74s for Chicken momo emphasis, 19.66s for profile arrival, and 22.93s for the final focus pull; land the logo on the 23.46s beat so the completed lockup still holds for more than 1.1 seconds
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle only; a low-opacity warmth layer may breathe with the music if supported, with no waveform, equalizer, particles, or text pulsing
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched; low-risk clicks, soft wood/soft-impact sounds, and one restrained final bell
- Audio-coupled moments: QR lock, Browse menu tap, modifier selection, add to cart, place-order success, request-bill confirmation, history row placement, logo landing
- Restraint rule: never score every row, never use bright casino pings, and never let sound compete with readable UI
