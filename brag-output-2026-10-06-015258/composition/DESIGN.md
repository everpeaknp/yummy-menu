# Yummy launch film design

The canvas is warm and quiet. A single large mobile device is the visual anchor. Product actions are shown in sequence inside the device, using the existing Yummy orange only for primary actions, selection, and success/status feedback.

Typography uses Outfit for short narrative headings and Inter for interface copy. The phone screen is dense enough to feel like a real consumer product while remaining legible at 1920×1080.

Transitions are contained inside the phone. Full-canvas wipes, persistent guide lines, decorative gradients, oversized editorial headings, and empty intermediate states are prohibited.
