# Yummy Menu Video Identity

## Style Prompt
Warm premium hospitality with operational clarity. Build from Yummy Menu's real visual language: a warm stone canvas, deep ink restaurant surfaces, purposeful orange actions, generous editorial spacing, compact menu rows, and food imagery that feels immediate rather than decorative. Motion should connect a real dining journey and then settle long enough to understand it.

## Colors
- Warm canvas: `#f6f6f4`
- Paper surface: `#fffdf9`
- Ink: `#10131a`
- Secondary text: `#57534e`
- Yummy orange: `#ff6929`

## Typography
- Display: Outfit, because it is the product's established display face.
- Body: Inter, because it is the product's established body face.
- This intentionally preserves the live product identity instead of substituting a video-template font pairing.

## Motion
- Confident ease-out entrances followed by readable holds.
- Horizontal carries for the working-product flow.
- One gentle blur crossfade into the return/rewards resolution.
- Sparse orange focus movement; no constant ambient zoom.

## What NOT to Do
- No gradients, particles, confetti, or generic SaaS glow.
- No invented metrics, prices, testimonials, restaurants, or customer data.
- No card wall; use dividers and grouped operational surfaces.
- No orange except for action, selection, and the final brand landing.
- No rapid text replacement that compromises reading time.
