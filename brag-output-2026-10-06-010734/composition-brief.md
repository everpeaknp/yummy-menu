# Composition brief

## Product

Yummy Menu is the customer-facing table experience for a restaurant visit: scan a table, browse the menu, customize an item, place an order, request service, and retain the visit in a customer profile.

## Audience and job

Restaurant guests using a phone at the table. The film must make the flow understandable without narration.

## Visual direction

- Warm off-white environment with a dark phone chassis.
- Yummy orange is an interaction color, never a transition bar.
- Poppins-like geometry is represented with the local Outfit display face and Inter for UI copy.
- The phone remains in one stable position so the causal flow is obvious.
- Left-side copy is short and subordinate to the working product.

## Non-goals

- No desktop dashboard imitation.
- No invented reward balance.
- No decorative cross-frame lines.
- No blank loading/menu state used as a transition.
- No changes to the application source or product contracts.
