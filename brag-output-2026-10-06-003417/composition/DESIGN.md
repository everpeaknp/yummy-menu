# Yummy Menu Product Film Design

## Intent
Warm, premium hospitality with operational clarity. The film shows one guest journey rather than isolated product features.

## Tokens
- Canvas: `#f6f6f4`
- Ink: `#10131a`
- Brand orange: `#ff6929`
- Paper: `#fffdf9`
- Stone divider: `#d6d3d1`
- Muted copy: `#5f5c57`
- Display: Outfit
- Body: Inter

## Composition
Use close, readable mobile-product crops with enough surrounding context to understand each action. Rounded surfaces, compact rows, dark restaurant header, orange focus states, and stone dividers come from the source application.

## Motion
The orange journey line is causal. It starts as scanner corners and carries through category underline, add control, modifier ring, cart badge, order status, bill status, points circle, and final logo dot. Buttons compress to 97–98%. Entrances ease out; positional transitions ease in-out.

## Restraint
No gradients, glassmorphism, particles, confetti, bright floods, fake 3D, decorative feature cards, fabricated product results, or generic SaaS copy.
