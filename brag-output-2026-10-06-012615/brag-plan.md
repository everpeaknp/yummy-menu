# Brag Plan: Yummy Menu — Complete Mobile Restaurant Visit

## What is this app?

Yummy Menu keeps a dine-in customer connected to the restaurant from table scan through ordering, receipt, service, points, and rewards.

## The angle

One true mobile visit. Every state is caused by the previous action and stays inside a phone-proportioned viewport.

## Hook

A real, scannable Table 8 QR starts the visit.

## User flow worth showing

Scan Table 8 → browse and customize Chicken momo → place the table order → review the ordered item and receipt → request the bill → see the saved visit, points, and rewards.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm hospitality with operational clarity
- Interpretation: calm camera, compact real UI, short readable copy, and restrained local motion.

## Format and duration

- Landscape: 1920×1080
- Duration: exactly 25 seconds
- Product viewport: true mobile proportions, approximately 430×932 CSS pixels

## Demo data

## Storyboard

1. **0.0–2.5 — Valid table QR**: phone scans a generated, decodable Table 8 QR.
2. **2.5–4.8 — Connected home**: verified Table 8 state and Browse menu.
3. **4.8–7.6 — Populated menu**: three momo rows appear immediately; Chicken momo is selected.
4. **7.6–10.2 — Customize**: Extra spicy is selected and added.
5. **10.2–13.4 — Cart and send**: active table, item, total, Place Order, Sending Request.
6. **13.4–16.3 — Current order**: ordered item, Active status, Receipt action, ordered total, grand total.
7. **16.3–18.4 — Receipt**: Order #1842, itemized estimate, paid and due values.
8. **18.4–20.7 — Table service**: four service actions remain visible; Request Bill enters cooldown.
9. **20.7–23.2 — Populated profile**: 1,240 points, 6 visits, two rewards, and saved order data.
10. **23.2–25.0 — Brand lockup**: clean Yummy Menu close.

## Audio direction

Low warm music bed, restaurant ambience at the opening, and restrained tap/status accents only.
