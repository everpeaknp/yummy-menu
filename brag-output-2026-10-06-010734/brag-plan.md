# Yummy Menu — Corrected 25-second launch film

## Strongest correction

The original film broke continuity by presenting unrelated desktop/editorial layouts and by carrying a full-width orange line between scenes. This version uses one persistent phone as the spatial anchor. Every action visibly causes the next state inside that same phone.

## Storyboard

1. **0.0–2.8 — Scan the table**  
   A compact table scene establishes the QR entry point. The phone scans Table 8.
2. **2.8–5.8 — Connected home**  
   The same phone settles into a fixed position. `Table 8 · connected` and `Browse menu` are visible.
3. **5.8–9.2 — Browse the actual menu**  
   The phone screen slides to a populated menu. Chicken momo, Fry momo, and Jhol momo are visible immediately.
4. **9.2–12.5 — Customize**  
   A bottom sheet rises inside the phone. `Extra spicy` is selected and `Add to Cart` is tapped.
5. **12.5–16.4 — Place the order**  
   The cart shows the selected item and table. `Place Order` becomes `Sending Request`, then `Order sent`.
6. **16.4–19.8 — Request the bill**  
   The connected table-service screen appears. `Request Bill` changes to `Request Bill · 5:00`.
7. **19.8–23.2 — Keep the visit**  
   The profile screen shows Orders, Yummy points, and Rewards without inventing balances.
8. **23.2–25.0 — Brand lockup**  
   A clean Yummy logo and `One connected restaurant visit.` close the film.

## Motion and visual rules

- 1920×1080, 25 seconds, 30 fps.
- One persistent phone from connected home through profile.
- Screen changes use contained horizontal slides; no full-frame wipe.
- No continuous orange line or cross-frame orange graphic.
- Orange appears only on local controls, selected states, and statuses.
- No empty menu frame and no oversized editorial page layouts.
- Music is low and secondary; taps use restrained local UI sounds.
