# Brag Plan: Yummy Menu

## What is this app?
Yummy Menu is a customer-facing restaurant experience for browsing menus, connecting to a table, ordering, requesting service, and keeping rewards and order history together.

## The angle
One restaurant visit moves through a single calm, connected surface: discover the place, choose a dish, connect the table, and return for rewards. The film should feel like hospitality software made for a real dining moment, not a generic delivery app.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on the Yummy orange mark and the verified product line: “Everything for your visit, in one place.” A dark restaurant surface resolves behind it, making the statement feel immediate and tangible.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A restaurant home transitions into “Browse menu,” then the real menu hierarchy appears with food imagery and NPR pricing.
- A dish is added and customized, then the table state and order total become visible.
- The customer profile reveals orders, points, rewards, and restaurant updates as the return loop.

## Outro / punchline
Land on “Dine, earn, return.” followed by the Yummy name and orange mark.

## User flow worth showing
Open a restaurant → browse and customize a dish → connect the table and place the order. Rewards and history close the loop without interrupting anonymous menu browsing.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm premium hospitality, operationally real
- Interpretation: restrained pacing, clean product-focused transitions, generous holds, and orange used only for decisive moments.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f6f6f4`
- Accent: `#ff6929`
- Text: `#10131a`
- Display font: Outfit
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the dark restaurant hero flowing into the light menu surface, with orange actions and real food imagery.

## Share copy (draft)
Meet Yummy Menu: browse, order from your table, call the floor team, and keep every reward and visit together.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, polished bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: use the opening 20 seconds at a restrained level, with a gentle fade-in and a clean fade beneath the final Yummy lockup
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at 114.84 BPM; target major reveals near 3.70s, 8.44s, and 20.02s; use every other beat for readable sequential menu/profile elements
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; allow the dark hero warmth and orange focus glow to breathe slightly with music energy, without waveform graphics or visible pulsing
- SFX posture: sparse, low-risk interface taps and one restrained final landing
- Audio-coupled moments: browse-menu action, dish customization selections, table connection, and final logo landing
- Restraint rule: no sound on every element and no bright casino-style cues

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — One place — 3.7s
The Yummy logo and orange mark enter over the dark restaurant canvas. “Everything for your visit, in one place.” settles at full readability, followed by the small “Dine, earn, return” pill.
Sequential/interaction: yes — logo, headline, then supporting pill; headline holds for at least 1.8 seconds
Audio intent: establish warmth and confidence without sounding corporate
Audio-coupled idea: soft logo arrival and a restrained accent as the headline settles
Music: warm polished bed
Transition mood: soft dark-to-light slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Browse the restaurant — 4.74s
Recreate the restaurant experience home and let the “Browse menu” action carry into the menu view. Real Yummy food imagery, category labels, dish names, and NPR prices appear as a working product surface.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate the Browse menu action, then reveal two or three dish rows on alternating beats and hold the completed menu
Audio intent: make the product feel responsive and inviting
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet tap followed by muted card arrivals
Music: warm polished bed
Transition mood: smooth product-surface wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — From dish to table — 6.32s
Select a dish, reveal its customization drawer, confirm it, and show the table-order state with an updating NPR total. Briefly expose the real service actions: Call Waiter, Request Bill, Water, and Cutlery.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated add, modifier selection, confirm, then table total; service actions arrive together and hold
Audio intent: convey useful momentum while preserving operational clarity
Audio-coupled idea: soft selection clicks, a grounded confirmation, and a restrained numeric settle
Music: warm polished bed
Transition mood: clean orange-accented wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Return is part of the visit — 5.24s
The customer profile surface reveals Orders, Rewards, Yummy points, and restaurant updates. Finish on the Yummy mark with “Dine, earn, return.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — orders and rewards settle first, then the final lockup lands near 20.02s
Audio intent: resolve with familiarity and trust
Audio-coupled idea: two soft profile arrivals and one final logo landing
Music: fade gently beneath the final mark
Transition mood: soft resolve to final hold
